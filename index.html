PRESERVE THE LUSTRE

Jewellery Care Guide

Simple daily habits ensure your precious silver keeps its radiant shine for generations.

✨ 925 Sterling Silver Care

  • Store Airtight: Keep your silver in an airtight zip-lock pouch or anti-tarnish box when not in use.
  • Avoid Chemicals: Apply perfumes, lotions, and hairspray before putting on your jewellery. Avoid pool chlorine and salt water.
  • Gentle Polishing: Buff gently with a soft microfiber silver polishing cloth. Avoid abrasive materials.
  • Wear Often: Pure silver benefits from regular wear as natural skin oils help prevent oxidation.

🥻 Festive Sarees & Handloom Drapes

  • Dry Clean Only: Handcrafted silks, tussar, and organza sarees should always be professionally dry cleaned.
  • Breathable Storage: Fold and store draped sarees wrapped inside a soft muslin or pure cotton bag.
  • Careful Ironing: Iron on low-to-medium heat with a protective cotton cloth over delicate zari borders.
  • Aerate Periodically: Unfold and air dry your sarees in a shaded, well-ventilated area every few months.

👑 Statement Festive Jewellery

  • Keep Dry: Wipe gently with a dry cotton swab after wearing to remove traces of sweat or cosmetics.
  • Store Separately: Keep intricate haslis, earrings, and bangles in separate compartments to avoid scratching.
  • Last On, First Off: Make statement jewellery the final addition to your outfit and the first thing you remove.
GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about WishRite jewellery, shipping, coupons, and orders.

What is 925 sterling silver? +

925 sterling silver is an authentic precious metal alloy composed of 92.5% pure silver and 7.5% copper or strengthening elements. This optimal ratio ensures your jewellery has radiant lustre, structural durability for everyday wear, and long-lasting beauty. Every WishRite silver piece is stamped with genuine hallmark certification.

How should jewellery be cared for? +

Store your pieces in airtight pouches away from humid environments. Always apply perfumes, sprays, and cosmetics before wearing your jewellery, and gently wipe each item with a soft microfiber cloth after use.

How does shipping work? +

Orders are dispatched within 24–48 hours from our central fulfillment facility in Kolkata (PIN 700092). We partner with premium courier services via Shiprocket (including Blue Dart, Delhivery, and DTDC). Metros receive shipments within 3–5 business days, and all other Indian locations within 4–7 business days.

Is Cash on Delivery (COD) available? +

Yes, Cash on Delivery is available across 26,000+ pin codes in India. For Silver Jewellery, a standard ₹50 COD verification charge is applied. For our active Occasion Collection, COD is 100% FREE!

What is the free shipping threshold? +

Silver Jewellery orders qualify for FREE shipping on order subtotals of ₹2,999 and above. For our Festive & Occasion Collection, FREE shipping unlocks on orders starting from just ₹199.

How do coupons work? +

You can enter any valid coupon code in the coupon input box in your shopping bag or during checkout, then click APPLY. Eligible discounts (percentage, fixed amount, or free delivery) will automatically deduct from qualifying items in your cart based on coupon scope (Jewellery, Occasion, or Storewide).

How can I track my order? +

As soon as your package is dispatched, we send you an automated tracking SMS and email containing your live Shiprocket AWB tracking link. You can track your shipment live at any time until it arrives safely at your doorstep.
