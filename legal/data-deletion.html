User Data Deletion Instructions

Learn how to request deletion of your WishRite account, remove associated social data, or revoke Facebook and Instagram permissions.

Policy Updated: November 13, 2026 Compliance: Meta Platform Data Deletion Guidelines & DPDP Act Support: info@wishrite.com

Your Right to Data Deletion: WishRite respects your digital privacy. In compliance with the Meta Platform Terms, Instagram Graph API guidelines, and the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act of India, you may at any time request the deletion of your personal data, customer history, creator profile, and connected social media tokens.

1. How to Disconnect Meta / Instagram from WishRite

If you linked your Instagram account to the WishRite Influencer Manager and wish to stop syncing data immediately without contacting support, you can disconnect directly:

  1. Directly in the WishRite Creator Dashboard

    Log in to your account at www.wishrite.in/creator, open your Creator Dashboard, scroll to the Instagram Connection card, and click Disconnect Instagram. This immediately clears your stored access tokens from the database and terminates active metrics syncing.

  2. Through Your Facebook Account Settings

    You can revoke WishRite's access directly from Meta's dashboard:

    • Go to your Facebook profile Settings & Privacy → Settings.
    • Under Permissions, navigate to Business Integrations (or Apps and Websites).
    • Locate WishRite or WishRite Influencer Manager.
    • Click Remove to permanently revoke all permissions granted to WishRite.

2. Categories of Data Subject to Deletion

Upon a verified data deletion request, WishRite purges the following data categories across our Supabase databases and application cache:

  • Connected Social Media Data: Encrypted Instagram and Facebook Page OAuth access tokens, Meta account IDs, social profile avatars, Instagram handle linkages, and cached media reach/engagement metrics.
  • Creator Profile Records: Creator Club application details, personal bio, contact phone number, address, and vanity storefront slugs.
  • Authentication & Customer Records: Supabase Auth user identity, email address, password hashes, and notification preferences.

Statutory Retention Exception: In accordance with Indian accounting, taxation (Goods and Services Tax / Income Tax Act), and anti-fraud regulations, financial records associated with completed e-commerce purchase orders, tax invoices, and disbursed commission payouts must be securely archived for the statutory retention period (typically 7–8 years) and cannot be immediately expunged.

3. How to Submit a Formal Data Deletion Request

To request a comprehensive deletion of your account and all associated personal data from our servers, follow these simple steps:

✉️ Submit via Email to Data Protection Team

Send an email from your registered email address with the following details:

To: info@wishrite.com
Subject: Data Deletion Request – WishRite Influencer Manager

Include in your message:

  • Your full registered name
  • Your registered account email address
  • Your Instagram handle (if you linked social accounts)
  • Specific scope (e.g., "Delete my creator profile and Instagram tokens" or "Permanently delete entire WishRite customer account")

4. Request Verification & Processing Workflow

To protect user accounts from unauthorized or malicious deletion requests, we follow a rigorous verification and completion workflow:

  • Step 1: Receipt & Tracking ID: Our team acknowledges your request within 2 business days and issues an internal reference code.
  • Step 2: Identity Verification: We confirm that the request originates from the email address registered on the account. In certain cases, we may request a one-time OTP confirmation.
  • Step 3: Database Purge: The administrator executes the server-side purge in our Supabase environment:
    • Clearing all encrypted tokens (AES-256-GCM tokens purged).
    • Removing influencer metadata, affiliate links, and campaign content associations.
    • De-identifying or deleting the authenticated customer profile.
  • Step 4: Written Confirmation: Within 14 business days of verification, you will receive a formal confirmation email verifying that your data has been permanently deleted from active systems.
