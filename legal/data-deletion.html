User Data Deletion Instructions

You can request deletion of personal information associated with WishRite, including information connected to the WishRite Influencer Manager and authorized Meta or Instagram integrations.

Updated: November 13, 2026Operator: Preeti Deepak Kumar — WISHRITE ProprietorshipCountry: India

1. How to request deletion

To request deletion of data associated with your WishRite account or your use of the WishRite Influencer Manager, send an email to our support team from the email address associated with your account.

Send your request to:

support@wishrite.com

Suggested subject:

Data Deletion Request — WishRite

Please include:

  • Your full name
  • The email address associated with your WishRite account
  • Your Instagram handle or Meta account identifier, if relevant
  • Whether you are requesting deletion of Meta/Instagram data, your creator account, or your full WishRite account

Email a deletion request

2. What happens after you contact us

We will review your request and may ask for reasonable information to verify that you control the account. After verification, we will identify the WishRite-held personal information covered by the request and process deletion where applicable. We may contact you if we need clarification or additional verification.

This email process submits a request for review; it does not itself perform an automatic or instant deletion.

3. Data that may be covered

Depending on the services you used and the scope of your request, this may include:

  • Meta or Instagram account identifiers and connection information associated with your WishRite account
  • Instagram profile information and insights received through permissions you authorized
  • Creator profile information, campaign submissions, and related account data
  • WishRite account information and associated personal information

4. Information we may need to retain

Some information may be retained where necessary to meet legal, tax, accounting, fraud-prevention, security, or dispute-resolution obligations. Where retention is required, we will limit use of that information to the applicable purpose and retain it for the required period.

5. Revoking Meta or Instagram access

You can also revoke permissions previously granted to WishRite through the relevant settings in your Facebook or Instagram account. Revoking permissions prevents future access through those permissions, but it does not by itself send a deletion request for information already held by WishRite. To request deletion of WishRite-held data, please email us using the instructions above.
