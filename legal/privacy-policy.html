Privacy Policy

How WishRite collects, handles, safeguards, and respects your personal and social media information.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with WishRite, we collect the following categories of information:

A. Customer Information

  • Contact & Delivery Details: Full name, shipping destination address, contact phone number, delivery postal PIN code, and email address.
  • Order History: Product items ordered, purchase timestamp, order amount, and logistics tracking reference numbers.

B. Creator & Influencer Information

  • Profile Details: Display name, contact email, phone number, bio, city, state, and designated vanity storefront handle.
  • Meta / Instagram Data (With Explicit Permission): When you authorize the WishRite Influencer Manager via Facebook Login for Business, we receive your public Instagram username, profile picture URL, professional account ID, follower counts, and organic performance metrics (reach, impressions, views, likes, comments) for submitted WishRite campaign content.

3. Secure Payment Processing (Zero Stored Financial Credentials)

WishRite does NOT collect, view, or store your credit card numbers, debit card numbers, UPI PINs, or net banking passwords. All online financial transactions are processed securely through Razorpay, an RBI-authorized payment aggregator operating with 256-bit SSL encryption and full PCI-DSS Level 1 compliance.

4. Third-Party Data Sharing & Operational Partners

We share limited, relevant data with trusted operational service providers strictly to perform essential functions:

  • Logistics Providers (Shiprocket & Associated Couriers): We share the recipient's name, destination shipping address, phone number, and delivery PIN exclusively for physical shipment and delivery tracking.
  • Database & Cloud Infrastructure (Supabase): Encrypted customer and creator accounts, wallet ledger entries, and referral statistics are hosted in secure, isolated database environments.
  • Meta Platforms (Facebook & Instagram Graph APIs): We interact with Meta's developer APIs strictly to verify professional account ownership and sync campaign metrics as authorized by the creator.

5. Token Security & Encryption Standards

Meta OAuth access tokens received during creator account authorization are protected by server-side AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption using strong server-side keys. If server-side encryption credentials are unavailable or an encryption error occurs, token persistence fails closed immediately—new tokens are never stored in plaintext, never exposed to frontend code, never passed in query parameters, and never logged in client consoles. Any legacy tokens encountered in the system undergo automated migration to AES-256-GCM encryption upon retrieval or creator reconnection. Decryption occurs exclusively in isolated server-side routines immediately prior to scheduled metrics synchronization.

6. Data Retention & User Deletion Rights

You have full ownership and control over your personal data:

  • You may request complete account deletion or the purging of connected Meta data at any time by following our Data Deletion Instructions or emailing info@wishrite.com.
  • You may immediately disconnect your Instagram profile at any time directly inside the Creator Dashboard or via Facebook Business Integrations settings.
  • Non-financial creator data and tokens are purged within 14 business days upon verified request. Statutory financial invoices are retained as required by Indian tax legislation.

7. Grievance Redressal & Inquiries

For questions or privacy concerns, please contact our Data Governance Desk at info@wishrite.com.
